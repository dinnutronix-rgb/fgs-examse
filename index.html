Before you begin

This is a supervised online examination. Your teacher watches your camera and microphone live during the test. Nothing from the camera or microphone is recorded, except your answers in the Speaking paper.

Stay in full screen on this tab for the whole paper. Switching tab or window, leaving full screen, copying or pasting, using shortcut keys or connecting a second screen will pause your test immediately. Only your teacher can let you continue.

Each paper is saved only when you finish it. If you leave a paper part-way through, your answers in that paper are discarded and you must start it again.

Camera and microphone Not checked

Choose Allow when your browser asks. Then say a few words to test the microphone.

Speakers or headphones Not checked

You need sound for the Listening paper. Play the test sound, then confirm you heard it.

Single screen Not checked

Only one screen may be connected during the test.

Full screen Not checked

The test runs in full screen.
